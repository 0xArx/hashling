Then you just ask. "Am I safe?" "What does this warning mean?" "Is this text a scam?"

Connect Claude with one command and it checks your Mac through Hashling, then answers from real data. It can only look and suggest. Every fix waits for your Touch ID.

**5**
Don't want your questions going to the cloud? Turn on Fully local mode.

Hashling picks a free open model that fits your Mac's memory, downloads it, checks it and runs it with llama.cpp. Nothing you ask leaves your Mac.

**6**

How much each app uploaded and to where, a heads-up when something talks to a new destination, where every download came from and who signed it, and API keys or secrets sitting in plain text.
