## First comment (founder's voice)

Hi Product Hunt. I am Abdulrahman, the solo developer behind Hashling.

I built it because AI coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) have surprisingly broad access to your Mac, and there was no easy way to see what each one was allowed to do. That became the starting point for a broader personal security tool.

Hashling lives on your Mac, watches the usual security things in plain words (network, malware, startup items, privacy permissions, scam check, breach watch), and adds something new: your AI can check your Mac's security for you. Run one command, and asking "is my Mac safe?" in Claude Code actually queries your Mac before it answers. Every fix still needs your Touch ID.

A few things I am honest about:
- It does not block connections live. Pair it with LuLu or Little Snitch for that.
- Malware is caught a few seconds after files arrive, not before they run. Malwarebytes does real-time interception and has lab testing. Hashling does not yet.
- No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything stays on your Mac.
