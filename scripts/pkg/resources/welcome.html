Hi, I’m Hashling.

I’m a free, open-source security helper for your Mac. I keep watch for scams, malware and AI apps that overstep, explain everything in plain words, and change nothing until you say yes with Touch ID.

This installer:

  • puts me in the Applications folder inside your home folder, so it doesn’t need your Mac password;
  • brings everything I need with it, so there’s nothing else to download or set up;
  • starts me and opens my dashboard for a 2-minute setup.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. No account, no tracking.
