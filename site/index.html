What it does for you

Is this a scam?

Paste a text, email or link, or drop in a screenshot. Hashling points out the tricks before you click, call or pay.

  • Checks links against known scam sites
  • Reads screenshots on your computer

Keeps your AI tools in check

AI assistants can run programs and read your files. Hashling shows what each one is allowed to do and warns you when one acts strangely.

  • Claude, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and more
  • Finds passwords left in their settings

Ask it anything

Is anyone tracking me? Why is my Mac slow? Hashling checks your computer first, then answers in plain words using the AI you already have.

  • Every warning has an Explain button
  • Simple, Standard or Expert detail

Your phone, too

Plug a phone in by cable and Hashling checks it for stalkerware, hidden profiles and apps from outside the store. Or pair it over Wi-Fi to carry Hashling in your pocket.

  • iPhone and Android
  • Only reads, never changes the phone

It also watches malware, startup items, trackers, downloads, uploads and your settings. See everything it checks

You stay in control

  1. 1

    It looks

    Hashling checks your apps, network and files. Looking never changes anything.

  2. 2

    It suggests a fix

    You see exactly what would happen, and can ask why, before anything runs.

  3. 3

    You say yes

    Approve with Touch ID. Most fixes can be undone, and clean-ups go to the Trash.

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Your data stays on your computer unless you ask an AI for help.
