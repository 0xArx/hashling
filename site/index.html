What it does for you

Is this a scam?

Paste a text, email or link, or drop in a screenshot. Hashling points out the tricks before you click, call or pay.

  • Checks links against known scam sites
  • Reads screenshots on your computer

Keeps your AI tools in check

AI assistants can run programs and read your files. Hashling shows what each one is allowed to do and warns you when one acts strangely.

  • Claude, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and more
  • Finds passwords left in their settings

Ask it anything

Is anyone tracking me? Why is my Mac slow? Hashling checks your computer first, then answers in plain words using the AI you already have.

  • Every warning has an Explain button
  • Simple, Standard or Expert detail

Your phone, too

Plug a phone in by cable and Hashling checks it for stalkerware, hidden profiles and apps from outside the store. Or pair it over Wi-Fi to carry Hashling in your pocket.

  • iPhone and Android
  • Only reads, never changes the phone

Why you can trust Hashling

A security app sees a lot of your computer, so you should not have to take our word for anything. Here is what you can check for yourself.

  • Open source

    Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license. Anyone can read what it does.

  • The AI can only look

    AI assistants connected to Hashling can read and suggest. They cannot change anything on your computer.

  • Touch ID for every change

    Nothing is removed, quit or blocked until you approve it with your fingerprint. Most changes can be undone.

  • No tracking

    No account, no analytics, no data sent home. Your files never leave your computer.

  • Signed and checked by Apple

    Each release has a copy of the app signed with a verified Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple, plus checksums so you can tell nothing was changed.

  • Graded in public

    OpenSSF Scorecard, from the Linux Foundation, grades how safely the project is run every week, and GitHub scans every change for security bugs.
