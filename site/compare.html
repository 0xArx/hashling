Hashling compared

An honest look at how Hashling sits alongside Little Snitch, LuLu, Malwarebytes, Bitdefender AI Guardian, and Snyk Agent Scan. Every tool listed here is good at what it does. They cover different things.

No single tool does everything. Hashling watches your Mac broadly in plain words, connects your AI to your security, and checks your phone. It does not block network connections in real time (Little Snitch and LuLu do that). It does not intercept malware the moment a file arrives (Malwarebytes does that). It is aimed at people, not CI pipelines.

If the honest trade-offs below suggest you need a tool Hashling does not cover, pair them. Hashling works well alongside a firewall like LuLu.

Each tool at a glance

Little Snitch

~$59 one-time license. Not open source.

Better than Hashling at

Blocking outgoing connections in real time, per app. When an app tries to call home, Little Snitch stops it and asks you. It is the gold standard for network-level privacy on a Mac. Also shows a detailed Network Monitor with traffic history.

Pick it when

You want to control which apps can reach the internet and you are comfortable managing a list of connection rules. It pairs well with Hashling.

LuLu

Free. Open source (by Objective-See).

Better than Hashling at

Blocking outgoing connections in real time, at no cost. Free alternative to Little Snitch from a trusted Mac security researcher. Fewer features, simpler UI, but the core firewall is solid.

Pick it when

You want live connection blocking for free. It pairs well with Hashling for broader visibility.

Malwarebytes for Mac

Free scan. ~$39.99/yr for real-time protection. Not open source.

Better than Hashling at

Blocking malware the moment a file arrives, before it can run. Independently tested by AV-Test, AVLab, and MRG Effitas. Hashling scans files shortly after arrival using ClamAV, YARA-X, and Apple's own XProtect rules.

Pick it when

You want lab-verified, real-time malware blocking and do not mind a subscription. Malwarebytes does not watch AI coding agents or explain things in plain words.

Bitdefender AI Guardian

Free during public beta (launched 30 Sep 2026). Not open source. Future pricing not announced.

Better than Hashling at

Intercepting and blocking individual AI agent actions before they run. AI Guardian acts as a gatekeeper between your AI coding agent and your Mac, blocking actions that break its policy. It also detects prompt injection attempts and verifies MCP tools before an agent calls them. Hashling watches and explains but does not block individual actions live.

Pick it when

You want a tool that actively blocks risky AI agent actions in real time. It currently supports Claude Code and OpenClaw only. It is in beta and future pricing is not announced.

Snyk Agent Scan

Free tier. $25+/user/month for teams. Open source (Apache-2.0). Requires a Snyk account.

Better than Hashling at

Deep supply-chain scanning of MCP servers, agent skills, and prompts for known vulnerabilities, tool poisoning, and hardcoded secrets. Built for software teams that want to audit their agent infrastructure in CI and across an organization. Snyk's broader platform integrates with code reviews, dependency scanning, and container security.

Pick it when

You are a developer or a security team that builds or uses AI agents and wants automated, policy-driven scanning in your pipeline. It is not designed for end users or as a personal Mac security agent.
